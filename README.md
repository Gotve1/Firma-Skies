## *Firma skies features*
</div>

### Realistic Survival
- True survival progression: knapping, pottery, metalworking, and forging
- Nutrition, temperature, thirst, and realistic food decay
- Geological world generation with authentic ore distribution
- Seasons affecting farming and survival strategy
- Early-game focus on craftsmanship and resource management

### Mechanical Automation
- Fully physical mechanical power systems (shafts, gears, belts)
- Conveyor-based processing lines
- Automated crushing, mixing, pressing, and washing
- Large-scale factory building with visual moving machinery

### Vehicle & Ship Engineering
- Build movable structures that function as ships or vehicles
- Physics-based flight and motion
- Kinetics-powered engines mounted on ships
- Mobile factories, airships, and transport platforms
- Multiplayer-ready moving bases

### Industrial Progression
- Gradual transition from manual crafting to automation
- Multi-stage metal tech tree
- water and wind-powered early mechanical setups
- Late-game advanced material production

### Engineering Challenges
- Weight and balance considerations for ships
- Power distribution planning for factories
- Resource logistics in a realistic world
- Environmental hazards and seasonal planning

### Expanded Farming & Ecology
- Crop rotation and soil nutrients
- Animal husbandry
- Greenhouses and irrigation automation
- Seasonal crop viability

### Exploration & Logistics
- Realistic world generation with temperature zones
- Airships/Vehicles/Ships for long-distance trade and exploration
- Moving storage depots and mining drills

### Gameplay Style
- Slow, rewarding progression
- Creative engineering freedom
- Visual, mechanical problem-solving
- Emergent player-built infrastructure

### Performance
- Singleplayer friendly
- 4GBs of ram required
- Well configured
- Works out-of-the-box
